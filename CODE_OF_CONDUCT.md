# PLIUM community code of conduct

PLIUM is an independently maintained open-source fork of Edge-Drop. Constructive bug reports, fixes, design feedback and respectful discussion are welcome.

Harassment, discriminatory conduct, personal attacks, exposure of sensitive information and abusive demands are not acceptable. Maintainers may remove or moderate inappropriate issues and comments.

When reporting issues, include reproducible steps and relevant environment details, **never** real clipboard history, personal information, passwords or access tokens. For sensitive issues, open a public issue requesting a private reporting route without disclosing confidential details.

This code applies to the PLIUM repository, not to the separate upstream Edge-Drop project. [PLIUM issues](https://github.com/ardss/PLIUM/issues) are the normal contact point.
